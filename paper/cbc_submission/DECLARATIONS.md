## Data availability

The raw data analysed in this study are publicly available from the NCBI Gene Expression
Omnibus under accessions GSE308103 (single-nucleus RNA sequencing) and GSE307534 (Visium
spatial transcriptomics).

All analysis code, together with the scripts that generate every figure in the manuscript,
is openly available at:

https://github.com/etonsalmon160-source/luad-invasion-v2

The repository also contains a provenance record that labels each analysis parameter by
its source (source paper, software default, or our own choice), and an audit of every
quantitative claim in the manuscript against the artifact that produced it. Large
intermediate objects are excluded from the repository because they are regenerable from
the code; the exclusion list records, for each omitted object, the script that rebuilds it.

*Elsevier usually asks authors to pick one of a small set of standard data-availability
categories. This text is written to match the category corresponding to "data available in
a public repository". If the submission system asks for a category rather than free text,
select that one and paste this text into the description field.*

## Ethics statement

This work is a secondary analysis of publicly available, de-identified human data
(GSE308103 and GSE307534), obtained from GEO. No new human specimens were collected, no
participants were recruited and no identifiable private information was accessed; ethical
approval and participant consent for the original data collection were obtained by the
studies that generated these datasets. Under our institutional policy, secondary analysis
of publicly available de-identified data does not constitute human-subjects research
requiring review, and no new ethical approval was therefore sought. No animals were used.
