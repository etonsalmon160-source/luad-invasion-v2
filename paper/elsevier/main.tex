\section*{Data availability}

The raw data analysed in this study are publicly available from the NCBI Gene Expression
Omnibus under accessions GSE308103 (single-nucleus RNA sequencing) and GSE307534 (Visium
spatial transcriptomics). All analysis code, including the scripts that generate every
figure, together with a per-parameter provenance record, is openly available at
\url{https://github.com/etonsalmon160-source/luad-invasion-v2}. Large intermediate objects
are excluded from the repository because they are regenerable from that code; the
exclusion list records, for each omitted object, the script that rebuilds it.

\section*{Ethics statement}

This work is a secondary analysis of publicly available, de-identified human data
(GSE308103 and GSE307534), obtained from GEO. No new human specimens were collected, no
participants were recruited and no identifiable private information was accessed; ethical
approval and participant consent for the original data collection were obtained by the
studies that generated these datasets. Under our institutional policy, secondary analysis
of publicly available de-identified data does not constitute human-subjects research
requiring review, and no new ethical approval was therefore sought. No animals were used.
